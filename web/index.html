LangCoach — web test build

Mirrors the iPhone app against your local backend. Hold the mic (or press and hold Space when the text field is empty) to talk; speech needs Chrome or Edge.

In this web build, speech recognition is done by the browser's speech service, not on device. The iPhone app transcribes on the phone.

Try this
  1. Say: type “me gusta escuchar este en mi mind”, then “como te va”, then “when do I use usted?”. Tap a word to see its pair.
  2. Capture: tap the capture pill, then Send as heard sentences. Today → See all shows them by hour.
  3. Scenes: search “dentist”, build a scene with details, open it, rate how ready you feel, then Rehearse. Answer once in Spanish and once in English.
  4. Library: search what you just built in other words, open a result, Use this scene.
  5. Review: Today → Start review. Reveal, rate, or hold the mic and say the Spanish.
  6. Progress and Settings (person icon on Today): change your variety, quiet hours, export or delete.

Two people? Open a private window and sign in with another name to see shared scenes from the other side.
