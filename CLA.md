# Intuye Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0, 15 November 2026

Thank you for contributing to Intuye. This agreement explains what you allow when you send a contribution, so that Intuye can stay free software and its maintainer can also offer it under other terms, for example a paid edition or builds in app stores.

In this agreement "you" means the person who sends the contribution, or the organisation they send it for. "The maintainer" means Ahsan Iqbal, and anyone the project is later handed over to. A "contribution" is any code, text, design, data or other material you send to the project, by pull request or otherwise.

## 1. Your contribution stays yours

You keep the copyright in your contribution. This agreement gives the maintainer a licence. It does not transfer ownership.

## 2. The licence you give

You give the maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, free and irrevocable licence to use, copy, change, publish, distribute and sublicense your contribution and works based on it, under any licence terms the maintainer chooses. That includes the GNU AGPL v3 or later, other open source licences, and closed or commercial terms, such as a paid edition of Intuye or builds sold or distributed through app stores.

## 3. Patents

If your contribution is covered by a patent you own or can license, you give the maintainer and everyone who receives Intuye from the maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, free and irrevocable patent licence to make, use, sell and distribute your contribution as part of Intuye. This licence ends for anyone who sues claiming that Intuye or your contribution infringes a patent.

## 4. What you confirm

1. The contribution is your own original work, or you have the right to send it under this agreement.
2. If your employer or anyone else has rights in work you create, they have allowed you to send it, or they have agreed to this agreement themselves.
3. Any part that comes from someone else is marked clearly, with its source and its licence.
4. You will tell the maintainer if any of this stops being true.

## 5. No promises either way

The maintainer does not have to use your contribution. You send it as it is, without warranties of any kind, unless you agree otherwise in writing.

## 6. The public project stays free

Intuye's public code stays available under the GNU AGPL v3 or later. This agreement does not limit what you can do with your own contribution.

## How to agree

Tick the agreement box in the pull request template. The pull request then serves as the record of your agreement for every contribution in it and in your later pull requests. If you send work for an organisation, say so in the pull request and confirm that the organisation agrees.

Each contribution is covered by the version of this agreement that was in force when you agreed. A later version applies to later contributions only if you agree to it again.
