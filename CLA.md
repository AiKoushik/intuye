# Intuye Contributor Licence Agreement

Version 1.0, 15 November 2026

Thank you for contributing to Intuye. This agreement explains what you give the project when you send a contribution, and what you keep. You sign it once, and it covers everything you contribute afterwards.

In this agreement, "you" means the person signing, and "the maintainer" means Ahsan Iqbal, who runs the Intuye project, together with anyone he transfers the project to. A "contribution" is anything you submit to the project, such as code, documentation, designs, translations or tests, through a pull request, an issue, a patch or any other channel the project uses.

## 1. You keep ownership

You keep the copyright in your contributions. You can use them for anything you like, including in other projects. This agreement gives the maintainer a licence. It does not transfer ownership.

## 2. The copyright licence you give

You give the maintainer, and everyone who receives Intuye from the maintainer, a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual and irrevocable licence to use, copy, modify, publish, distribute and sublicense your contributions and works based on them.

This licence includes the right to release your contributions under licences other than the GNU AGPL, including commercial and proprietary licences. In practice this lets the maintainer ship Intuye through app stores, sell a paid edition, and offer commercial licences to companies that cannot use the AGPL.

## 3. The patent licence you give

If you hold patents that your contribution would infringe, you give the maintainer, and everyone who receives Intuye from the maintainer, a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual and irrevocable licence to make, use, sell, offer to sell, import and otherwise transfer your contribution and Intuye with your contribution included. This licence ends for anyone who starts patent litigation claiming that Intuye or your contribution infringes a patent.

## 4. What you promise

You confirm that

1. each contribution is your own original work, or you have the right to submit it under this agreement,
2. if your employer or anyone else has rights in your work, they have allowed you to contribute it, or have waived those rights,
3. you will say clearly in the pull request if any part of a contribution comes from someone else, including code generated from material under another licence, and name its source and licence, and
4. you will tell the maintainer if any of these statements stops being true.

## 5. What the project promises in return

Every contribution the project accepts will stay available in the public Intuye repository under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later. The maintainer can offer Intuye under other terms as well, but cannot take your work out of the open version.

## 6. No obligations and no warranty

The maintainer does not have to use your contribution. You do not have to support it. Contributions are given as they are, without warranties of any kind, except the promises in section 4.

## 7. Transfer

The maintainer may transfer this agreement, along with the rights it grants, to a company or person that takes over the Intuye project. The promise in section 5 transfers with it.

## How to sign

The first time you open a pull request, a bot will ask you to sign. Reply on the pull request with this exact sentence.

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records your GitHub username, the date and the pull request. You will not be asked again.

If you contribute for a company, or would rather sign on paper, open an issue and the maintainer will send you a corporate version.
