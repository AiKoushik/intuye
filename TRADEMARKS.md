# The Intuye name and logo

The code is free software under the [GNU AGPL v3 or later](LICENSE). The name "Intuye", the slogan "Sense it. Say it." and the Intuye logo and app icon are not covered by that licence. They belong to Ahsan Iqbal.

You are welcome to

* say that your project is based on Intuye, or works with it,
* use the name in articles, talks, reviews and tutorials about Intuye, and
* run an unmodified copy of Intuye for yourself or your team under its own name.

Please don't

* publish a modified version, or a hosted service built on one, under the name Intuye or a name that could be confused with it,
* use the logo or icon for your own app, or
* suggest that your project is the official Intuye or is endorsed by it.

If you publish a fork, give it a new name and icon before you distribute it. Questions about other uses go to the maintainer through an issue.
